# Contributing Guide  

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **AI Designs a New World After the Extinction of Humanity** ✨  

This is an **open creative project**: a speculative sci-fi series exploring love, identity, and humanity rebuilt by AI.  
Contributions are welcome in many forms — writing, editing, illustration, translation, and more.  

---

## 🖊️ Ways to Contribute  

You can get involved in different ways:  

- **Ideas & Discussions**  
  Open an [Issue](../../issues) to suggest plot points, character arcs, or philosophical questions.  

- **Writing & Editing**  
  Contribute draft chapters, scene sketches, or refine existing text.  
  Submissions can be in **Markdown (.md)** for easy versioning.  

- **Illustrations & Visuals**  
  Concept art, cover ideas, character sketches — visuals help bring the story world alive.  

- **Translations**  
  Translate chapters, excerpts, or README files to make the project accessible in more languages.  

- **Worldbuilding & Research**  
  Add notes on science, philosophy, linguistics, or speculative technology.  

---

## 🌐 Workflow  

1. **Fork** this repository  
2. Create a new branch (e.g. `feature/add-chapter1-draft`)  
3. Add or edit content in Markdown or images in `/assets/`  
4. Submit a **Pull Request** with a clear description of your contribution  

---

## ✍️ Writing Guidelines  

- **Tone & Style**: Philosophical, emotional, reflective sci-fi.  
- **Perspective**: Multiple viewpoints (children + robotic parents).  
- **Themes**: Love, authenticity, humanity, identity, diversity.  
- **Length**: Contributions can be short fragments, full chapters, or essays.  

---

## 🎨 Visual Contributions  

- Place artwork in the `/assets/art/` folder.  
- Add alt-text (accessibility!) in the description.  
- Credit will always be given to the artist.  

---

## 🤝 Community Guidelines  

- Be respectful of all contributors.  
- Keep feedback constructive.  
- Diversity of ideas is welcome — alternative plotlines, rituals, or cultures can enrich the project.  

---

## 📖 Licensing  

All contributions will be released under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC-BY-SA)**.  
This ensures open collaboration while crediting each contributor.
